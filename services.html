What We Offer

Comprehensive support through every stage of the recovery and reintegration journey.

Core Offerings

  • Safe, structured residential accommodation
  • Addiction and mental health recovery support
  • Daily routines, house rules, and positive accountability
  • Life skills development and personal growth support
  • Regular care check-ins and wellbeing monitoring
  • Family communication and relationship rebuilding
  • Ongoing support through every stage of the journey

From Recovery to Employment — Every Step of the Way

At CFUC, recovery is not the finish line—it is the beginning. When a resident has shown genuine commitment, consistency, and personal growth, we walk with him into the next chapter:

01. Work-Readiness Assessment

We assess each resident individually—only when he is genuinely ready does the employment journey begin.

02. Active Job Placement Support

CFUC actively assists residents in finding employment—connections, preparation, and advocacy on their behalf.

03. Safe Transport & Ongoing Support

CFUC arranges safe, reliable transport to work and back—and remains a consistent support structure throughout.
